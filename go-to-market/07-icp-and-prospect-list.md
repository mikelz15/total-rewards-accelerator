# ICP & Prospect List — Who actually needs TRA

**Honest framing:** No public list proves a company “will buy.”  
What you *can* do is target orgs where **pain + process + budget + title** line up.  
This file = **who to hunt**, **how to find them**, and a **starter board** of high-fit *types* and example orgs to research—not a purchased lead file.

---

## 1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Firmographics
| Attribute | Sweet spot | Stretch | Avoid (first 90 days) |
|-----------|------------|---------|------------------------|
| Employees | **500–8,000** | 300–500 or 8–15k | &lt;200 (no process); 50k+ enterprise RFPs |
| Industry | **Healthcare systems**, multi-site services, mid-market corp | Tech with formal Comp, higher ed medical centers | Pure startups with no ranges |
| Comp function | Dedicated Comp / TR / Rewards | HRBP owning equity + merit | “HR generalist does everything” only |
| Systems | Workday / UKG / PeopleSoft **exports to Excel** | Mixed HRIS | Fully custom in-house comp platform (long cycle) |
| Trigger | Merit cycle, pay equity review, M&A grade alignment, retention crisis | New Comp hire “building the function” | “No budget until next FY” with no date |

### Buyer titles (priority order)
1. Compensation Manager / Director  
2. Total Rewards Manager / Director  
3. Manager/Director, People Analytics or HR Analytics (comp focus)  
4. HRBP Lead / Director HR Ops (if they own merit or equity)  
5. TA Compensation / Offer specialist (Closer module wedge)  
6. CHRO / VP People — **only** via intro or after Comp champion  

### Jobs-to-be-done (they feel these)
- “Merit is a hero spreadsheet every year.”  
- “We can’t explain who’s under mid vs who we should retain.”  
- “Pay equity request from legal/board and data is a mess.”  
- “TA only sells base; we lose candidates on total package.”  
- “M&A or restructure — grades and ranges don’t match reality.”  

### Why *your* product fits
Lean Comp teams (often **0–6** people under ~3,000 EE) do TR work in Excel.  
Orgs ~500+ EE are far more likely to have a TR/Comp owner.  
Healthcare: tight labor market + complex clinical/non-clinical pay = messy HRIS + equity pressure.

## 2. How to compile *your* live list (best methods)

You do **not** need a scraped mega-list. You need **80–120 named humans**.

### A. LinkedIn (highest ROI) — no special tools required
1. Search titles: `Compensation Manager`, `Total Rewards`, `Compensation Analyst`, `People Analytics`  
2. Filter: **2nd degree** first (warm path), location US (or your market)  
3. Open profiles → note **company + EE size** (Company page → About / Insights)  
4. Save to spreadsheet (template below)

**Sales Navigator** (if you pay ~1 month):  
- Title contains Comp or Total Rewards  
- Company headcount **501–5,000** and **5,001–10,000**  
- Industry: Hospital & Health Care, and 2–3 other verticals you know  
- Geography: your demo timezone ±  

### B. Warm graph (do this first — days 1–14)
Export mental Rolodex:
- Past employers’ Comp/TR/HRBP  
- Conference / WorldatWork / local HR contacts  
- Recruiters who place Comp people (they intro)  
- Healthcare leaders you’ve worked with  

**Target: 40 warm names before heavy cold outreach.**

### C. Company discovery (public)
| Source | How to use |
|--------|------------|
| LinkedIn company search | Healthcare + “hospital system” + size |
| Regional hospital associations | Member lists → find Comp contacts on LI |
| PE portfolio pages | Mid-market holdings often under-invested in Comp ops |
| Job posts | “Compensation Analyst” open req = active pain / budget |
| Your AngloGold / healthcare network | Adjacent intros |

### D. Signals a company is “hot” this quarter
- Hiring Comp Analyst / TR Manager  
- Recent merger or rebrand of system  
- Public pay-equity or retention press  
- Merit/bonus cycle timing (many Q1 or mid-year)  
- “Workday implementation” or HRIS migration (data chaos)

## 4. Starter target *segments* (build rows from these)

### Segment A — Regional / multi-hospital systems (best brand fit for you)
**Why:** Complex job families, clinical vs non-clinical, retention, messy exports.  
**Examples to research** (publicly known systems—not endorsements; verify Comp contact on LinkedIn):

| Example org type | Notes for outreach |
|------------------|--------------------|
| Regional nonprofit health systems (multi-hospital) | Look for Comp Director / TR |
| Academic medical centers (mid-size) | Analytics + equity pressure |
| Large community hospital networks | Merit + market adjustments |
| Specialty systems (behavioral, rehab, senior care chains) | Often Excel-heavy Comp |

**How to expand:** LinkedIn → Companies → “Hospital & Health Care” → 1,000–10,000 employees → your state + neighbors.

### Segment B — Mid-market employers with a real Comp seat
**Why:** ~500–5,000 EE often have Comp but thin tooling.  
**Look for:** manufacturing, logistics, retail home office, business services, insurance, credit unions, regional banks.

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Job post “Compensation Manager” | Message the hiring manager + peers at that company |
| “Total Rewards” on About page | Find named leader |
| Recent funding / PE ownership | Cost control + talent = merit/equity focus |

### Segment C — Post-M&A or multi-entity
**Why:** Grade structures and ranges collide; placement + equity pain is acute.  
**Find:** Press releases “acquires,” “joins,” “system merger” + Comp titles at acquirer.

### Segment D — TA-led wedge (Closer)
**Why:** Faster curiosity; smaller $ (workshop) possible.  
**Titles:** TA Comp, Offer Management, Recruiting Ops at companies with competitive hiring (healthcare TA, tech mid-market).  
**Path:** Workshop $2–3.5k → introduce equity/merit to Comp later.

### Segment E — Avoid for first revenue
- Big 4 / large consultancies (they sell competing advice)  
- Enterprise 50k+ without an internal champion (6–18 month security review)  
- Companies that only want free unlimited SaaS  
- Anyone insisting on live SSN/PHI in your public demo (compliance no)

## 5. Example “starter 25” research queue

Use this as a **homework list**: open LinkedIn, find *one* Comp/TR human per org, add to your sheet.  
Replace with local/regional names as you go. Categories &gt; obsessing over any single logo.

### Healthcare / care delivery (priority)
1. A multi-hospital system in **your metro or last employer’s region**  
2. Second regional system (competitor market)  
3. Large **FQHC / ambulatory** network with 500+ EE  
4. **Senior living / post-acute** chain (often under-tooled Comp)  
5. Behavioral health multi-site employer  
6. Academic medical center (Comp + Faculty affairs complexity)  
7. Children’s or specialty hospital system  
8. Health plan / payer with 1k–8k EE (different but Comp-heavy)  

### Mid-market non-healthcare
9. Regional bank / credit union (regulated pay, merit)  
10. Insurance carrier or TPA mid-size  
11. Manufacturing 1k–5k EE (shift differentials → messy data)  
12. Logistics / distribution employer  
13. National franchise corporate HQ  
14. PE-backed multi-location services company  
15. Higher-ed **system** office (not single tiny college)  

### Trigger-based (search this week)
16–20. Five companies with **open Comp/TR job posts** in last 30 days  
21–25. Five companies in your LinkedIn **2nd-degree** network at 500–8k EE with Comp titles  

**After 25 rows:** do not collect more until you’ve DMed the first 25 contacts.

## 8. Qualification score (0–10) before a long demo

Give 2 points each:
- [ ] EE in 500–8,000  
- [ ] Named Comp/TR (or clear owner)  
- [ ] Merit, equity, or offer pain **this or next cycle**  
- [ ] Can use **scrubbed** file or accept sample-first pilot  
- [ ] Budget path for **$3–6.5k** without 6-month RFP  

**≥6:** prioritize. **≤4:** nurture or workshop only.
