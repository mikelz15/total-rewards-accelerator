# 90-Day Revenue Plan — Total Rewards Accelerator

**North star (90 days):** first paid design partners, not SaaS scale.  
**Primary offer:** Design Partner Pilot **$3,000** (first 3) · **$4,500** standard · Workshop **$2,000–$3,500**.  
**Demo:** https://tra-web.onrender.com · password via DM only.

**Planning target (base case):** **$12,000–$18,000** closed in 90 days  
= e.g. **3 early pilots @ $3–4.5k** *or* **2 pilots + 1–2 workshops**.

| Case | Closed $ | What it takes |
|------|----------|----------------|
| Floor | $0–3k | 1 workshop or stall |
| **Base (plan to this)** | **$12–18k** | 8–15 demos, 2–4 paid |
| Stretch | $20–30k | 4–6 paid + strong referrals |

60 warm + 80 cold DMs → ~15 conversations → 12 demos → 6 proposals → **2–4 closes**.

## Ideal buyer (one sentence)

**Comp / Total Rewards / Rewards Analytics lead** at a **~500–8,000 employee** org (especially **healthcare**, multi-site, or post-M&A) who still runs **merit + equity in Excel/HRIS exports** and has a **cycle in the next 6 months**.

**Also buy:** People Analytics, HRBP lead owning pay equity, TA Comp partner for offers.  
**Rarely buy first:** CHRO at 50k+ EE (too political), or solo HR at &lt;150 EE (no budget/process).

See `07-icp-and-prospect-list.md` for company targeting.

## Demo → cash script (use every time)

**End of free demo (2 minutes):**
1. “Does this match a live pain this quarter — merit, equity, or offers?”  
2. If yes: “Design partner is fixed scope, **$3k early / $4.5k standard**, 4–6 weeks, I run it with you.”  
3. “I’ll send a 2-page SOW today. Any reason we wouldn’t start in the next 30 days?”  
4. Calendar: kickoff hold + invoice for 50% deposit if they say yes.

**Disqualify politely if:** no Comp owner, &lt;200 EE with no equity process, “just looking for free software forever,” or needs SOC2/SSO before talking (park for SaaS later).
