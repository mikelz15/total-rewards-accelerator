## 3. Who to say yes / no to

### Strong fit
- Has a near-term merit, equity, or offer pain  
- Can provide **scrubbed** data or use sample + synthetic overlay  
- Decision-maker or influencer in Comp/TR  
- Willing to give product feedback  

### Weak fit (politely waitlist)
- Wants unlimited enterprise SaaS tomorrow with SSO/SOC2 day one  
- Won’t scrub PII and insists on emailing full SSN files  
- “Can you just build our whole comp program for free for feedback?”  
- No budget *and* no path to budget in 90 days (unless strategic logo you choose)

- **50%** on signature / kickoff  
- **50%** at midpoint or final readout  
- Or **100%** upfront for early-bird $3,000 seats  
- Invoices Net 15

## 5. Data & security posture (pilot-honest)
State this clearly in writing:

1. Prefer **scrubbed files** (remove SSN, home address; hash or fake EE IDs if needed).  
2. Client remains **data controller**; you process only for pilot services.  
3. Files stored only for pilot duration; deleted within **30 days** after close (or sooner on request).  
4. No training of public models on client employee data.  
5. Tool is **early access** — not a substitute for legal pay-equity counsel.  
6. Demo environment may be hosted/shared access; not yet multi-tenant enterprise grade.

Mutual NDA-style language (or reference standalone NDA).  

### I. Data protection
See section 5; attach data handling addendum if needed.  

### J. Publicity
Default: no public case study without written approval.  
Optional checkbox: “Client allows anonymized LinkedIn case study.”

- Services performed professionally.  
- Software provided **as-is** for pilot.  
- Not legal, tax, or formal pay-equity certification.  

### L. Limitation of liability
Cap at fees paid in the pilot term (standard commercial language).  

### M. Termination
Convenience with notice; payment for work performed.  

### N. Design partner feedback
Client agrees to 1 short feedback survey + optional roadmap call.  

### O. Signatures
