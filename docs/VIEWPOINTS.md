## Credit, and the benchmark files

Full, half, and none follow a survey match.

The method is in the 2020 benchmark workbooks under Compensation History, not in a file named mapping:

- `Benchmark Base 4.3.20- MLL.xlsx`
- `Z Benchmark Review 6.2.20- MLL.xlsx`
- `Benchmarking Excercise 2020 Data.xlsx`
- `Final Z Benchmark Grades 6.9.20.xlsx`

A company job is matched to a benchmark job and a benchmark code. The June review also has family, function, and focus, plus a second title when the first match is soft. Notes that say the match is a grade off, or not confident, are half credit. The same benchmark code is full credit. A different family is none.

`Final Z Benchmark Grades` has a Years of Experience column on the job. That is how a matched job sits in the range. It is not the sum of someone's past jobs. The resume dates still produce the years. The match only sets the fraction.

The two `CompAnalyst Report out 10.5.19.xlsx` files, under Markets/2020 and Salary Survey/2019, are the same export: job code, title, family, description. They are the benchmark catalog the match sheets point at.

Those files stay in iCloud. Market rates, the company job catalog, and the survey descriptions do not go into the repo or the product. The sample tally uses a short family list written for the sample resume.
