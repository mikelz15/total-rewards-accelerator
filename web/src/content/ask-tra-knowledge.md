# What Total Rewards Accelerator is

Total Rewards Accelerator (TRA) is a compensation toolkit built by Mikéz
(Michael Lopez), a compensation strategist. The idea is simple: stop crunching
rows, start designing strategy. TRA is a set of modules for the comp cycle —
cleaning HR data, auditing pay equity, running a merit cycle, tracking
candidates, and closing offers. Modules can be bought separately or as a suite.

The workflow the modules follow is: Clean, Place, Audit, Lock, Close.

Placement in TRA is designed around one shared model — years of experience plus
education — so the modules speak the same language about where a person should
sit in a range.

# The tools

## Cleaner (Market Data Cleaner) — /cleaner

Cleaner is the module for messy HRIS exports. Its job is to turn an HRIS export
into analysis-ready records by mapping columns, money, and dates — including
years of experience and education. It is step 01 of the workflow: everything
downstream starts from a cleaned file.

## Equity + Merit (Pay Equity Auditor + Remediation) — /auditor

Equity + Merit is TRA's pay-equity and merit module — the audit and remediation
side of the suite. It is step 02 of the workflow. Cycle Lock and Philosophy
Check are included with Equity + Merit; they are not separate invoices. The
full suite has four invoices total.

## Cycle Lock — /cycle

Cycle Lock is the range-first merit step, included with Equity + Merit. On the
public demo it runs one fixed envelope — 3.2% — and spends it range-first:
ratings do not allocate the pool, under-range repair comes first, increases
decelerate toward the midpoint, nothing goes above midpoint, and a scarce
exception needs an explicit confirm.

## Philosophy Check — /philosophy

Philosophy Check asks one question: does the pay file match what you said you
pay for? It states your philosophy as three claims, written so they can fail —
one about the market midpoint band, one about performance, and one about
leftover department effects — and scores them. On the public page you can score
the claims on the sample file, or set the bands and run a synthetic file.

## Candidate Tracker — /candidates

Candidate Tracker is the recruiting pipeline: stages (sourced, screen,
interview, offer, accepted), offer packages, and notes — with a handoff into
Closer to generate a total-wealth statement for a candidate. It is a separate
tool from Closer. On the public demo, seeded personas illustrate the stage
tracking and the Closer handoff; full create, edit, and import unlock on a
design-partner pilot or a paid module. Never enter real candidate information
into the public demo.

## Closer (Candidate Closer) — /closer

Closer takes an offer's base salary, bonus, and LTI and turns them into a
four-year total wealth trajectory. It generates a one-page PDF that turns an
offer letter into a closing tool — the four-year view most market tools skip.
You enter the offer's numbers yourself.

Resume tally is on this same page. It is not its own module and not its own
invoice. Paste one resume and name the target job. Same benchmark is full
credit. Same family, or a match a grade off, is half. A different function is
none. Overlapping time counts once. Confirm fills Years of experience. The
homepage film is labelled Resume tally.

Closer does not compare an offer against your internal pay ranges or your
existing employees' pay, and it does not test offer scenarios against internal
equity. Questions about internal equity belong to the audit side of the suite.

# Datasets, Rules, and Muse

Datasets is the signed-in library for a cleaned file. The next module opens
that file. The public demo does not keep uploads.

Rules is the signed-in library for the places on that file. It is not a module.
A card chips when it is inside the time window.

Muse is a propose-only connector at /muse. It can propose a clean, a placement,
an audit, a pool, a four-year total, and a short brief. It does not post pay.

# The public demo and the sample file

The public demo runs on a sample file — a small, messy HRIS extract with
fictional people. The homepage shows the sample before and after cleaning: the
extract's columns (worker ID, legal name, total base pay annualized, pay range
midpoint) become the cleaned columns the tools keep (name, base salary, range
midpoint, years, education). The short films on the homepage use the sample
file.

Demo rules, as posted on the site:

- The demo prefers the sample file. Use "Load messy HRIS sample" on the
  Cleaner page.
- A custom upload or paste is capped at 10 data rows and limited to five per
  week from one address, and headers are scanned for sensitive fields (SSN,
  date of birth, address, phone, email, medical record numbers, and similar).
- Tracker and Closer stay on synthetic data in the demo.
- Do not upload a real employee or candidate file — not to the demo, and not
  into this chat. That rule has no exceptions.
